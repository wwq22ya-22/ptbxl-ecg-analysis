serial_samples.npz contains 128 records selected from official validation
fold 9 after excluding records with no positive diagnostic superclass.
The ecg_id array identifies the source records; IDs also appear in
deployment_manifest.json. This is a demonstration subset, not a new dataset.
raw_mv stores original waveforms in millivolts; ecg stores signals after
baseline removal, bandpass filtering and training-set global normalization.
targets stores the five diagnostic superclass labels. reference_probabilities
comes from the generated Cube.AI host C implementation, not a physical board.
validation_reference.npz contains the corresponding preprocessed validation
subset and FP32 reference outputs. No training or test-fold signals are
included in these demonstration arrays. The data's CC BY license does not
extend to the ST runtime or imply endorsement by the dataset authors.
