serial_samples.npz contains all 2158 eligible records from official fold 10,
excluding records with no positive diagnostic superclass, as in the seed44 training cohort.
ecg_id and patient_id identify source records; strat_fold is 10 for every row.
raw_mv stores original mV waveforms; ecg stores baseline removal, bandpass
filtering and normalization using frozen training-set statistics.
targets stores NORM/MI/STTC/CD/HYP multi-label targets. reference_probabilities
comes from a fresh Cube.AI host C-model validation, not a physical board.
sample_manifest.json binds this bundle to the existing seed44 model and thresholds.
Historical fold-10 results have been used during development; replay is not a new blind test.
